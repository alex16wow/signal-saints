Formed in Berlin in 2023, SIGNAL / SAINTS began as an unexpected collaboration between two musicians from different generations and musical backgrounds.

What started as a spontaneous studio session quickly evolved into something neither of them had planned. Combining X-LIZA’s emotional songwriting and powerful vocals with Jack Gun’s heavy rhythms and electronic production, the duo developed a sound that moves between alternative rock, industrial and electronic music.

Their 2026 release Fractures marks a new chapter in their journey, exploring the tension between chaos and control, connection and isolation.

Elizabeth VossVocals / Lyrics / Visual Direction

Elizabeth Voss grew up in Hamburg and found her voice in Berlin’s underground music scene. She began writing and recording songs in her bedroom, blending fragile melodies with distorted guitars and electronic sounds. As X-LIZA, she transforms personal experiences into raw, emotionally charged performances, bringing an unpredictable energy to SIGNAL / SAINTS.

Jonathan MercerDrums / Production / Sound Design / Backing Vocals

Born in Manchester, Jonathan Mercer spent over a decade touring and recording with alternative and industrial rock bands. His growing interest in electronic music eventually led him into production and sound design. Under the name Jack Gun, he combines powerful live drumming with intricate electronic textures, bringing structure, precision and experience to the duo’s sound.
